01 — Problem Statement

Trip planning is stressful because the work is scattered

Travelers searching for a single trip typically bounce across multiple sites to shortlist destinations, string together a day-by-day itinerary, estimate a realistic budget, and dig up the food and local spots worth actually visiting. The AI Trip Planner collapses that into one step: it takes a traveler's starting point, destination, trip length, and daily budget, and generates a personalized, ready-to-follow plan.

05 — Approach

What's actually generating this

Four inputs drive an LLM-based planning pipeline that grounds each day in real transport data and locally-sourced food picks rather than generic "top 10" lists.

1

Structured prompting

Origin, destination, days, and daily budget are turned into a constrained prompt so every day's cost stays inside the traveler's stated range.

2

Route-aware transport

Train, flight, and bus options are pulled in with real timings and fares so the "getting there" step isn't left as an afterthought.

3

Local-first food picks

Food recommendations favor named local institutions over generic "must-try" listicle spots.

4

Budget reconciliation

Daily estimates are summed and checked against the traveler's target so the plan is honest about whether it fits.

06 — What's Next

Known gaps, honestly

→

Live fare refresh

Transport costs are point-in-time snapshots today; next step is refreshing them at generation time so they don't go stale.

→

Multi-city trips

Currently single-destination only — chaining cities (e.g. Jaipur → Udaipur) is the next planning unit to support.

→

Seasonal awareness

Itineraries don't yet adjust activity order/timing for extreme heat or monsoon — a clear near-term improvement.

→

Booking hooks

Right now it's plan-only; connecting to actual booking flows for transport/hotels is the logical next layer.
